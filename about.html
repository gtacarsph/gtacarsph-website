About GTACarsPH

Your trusted partner in quality pre-owned vehicles and Ford expertise — serving Metro Manila since 2015.

Our Story

Building Trust,
One Drive at a Time

GTACarsPH was founded with a simple mission — to change the way people buy and sell used cars in Metro Manila. We believed that every transaction should be built on honesty, transparency, and genuine care for the customer.

Starting from a small shop in San Juan, we grew into one of the area's most trusted automotive dealerships. Our secret? We treat every customer like family. Whether you're buying your first car, upgrading to a newer model, or selling your vehicle — we're here to guide you every step of the way.

Today, we're proud to be San Juan's #1 Ford specialist, with a dedicated team of automotive professionals who share our commitment to quality, integrity, and exceptional service.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

What drives us every day and where we're headed.

Our Mission

To provide every Filipino with access to quality, reliable pre-owned vehicles at fair prices — backed by honest advice, professional service, and a team that genuinely cares. We strive to make every car buying and selling experience simple, transparent, and stress-free.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted automotive dealership in Metro Manila — known not just for the cars we sell, but for the relationships we build. We envision a future where GTACarsPH is synonymous with integrity, expertise, and excellence in the Philippine automotive industry.
