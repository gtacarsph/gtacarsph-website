Privacy Policy

How GTACarsPH collects, uses, and protects your personal information

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. GTACarsPH is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our services, visit our website, or interact with us through our platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When you use GTACarsPH services, we may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information You Provide

  • Full name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Location details for vehicle pickup, inspection, or delivery
  • Vehicle details including make, model, year, plate number, and condition
  • Photos and videos of your vehicle for listing and appraisal purposes
  • Messages, inquiries, and communications sent through our website or social media pages
  • Payment information (processed securely through accredited payment partners — we do not store credit card details)

Information Collected Automatically

  • IP address, browser type, and device information when you visit our website
  • Pages viewed, time spent on site, and click patterns (via analytics tools)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 5 for details)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Transaction Processing: To facilitate the purchase, sale, or appraisal of vehicles
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, provide quotations, and assist with your transactions
  • Service Improvement: To analyze website usage and improve our services, listings, and user experience
  • Marketing: With your consent, to send promotional offers, new inventory alerts, and updates about GTACarsPH services (you may opt out at any time)
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes in the Republic of the Philippines

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely for GTACarsPH business operations and as described in this policy.

3. Information Sharing

We may share your personal information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party partners who help us operate our business (payment processors, courier services, vehicle inspection partners) — these providers are contractually bound to protect your data
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity
  • With Your Consent: Any other purpose for which you provide explicit consent

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted through our website
  • Secure server infrastructure with restricted access
  • Regular security audits and monitoring
  • Limited employee access to personal data on a need-to-know basis
  • Industry-standard malware and intrusion prevention systems

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will notify you of any data breaches in accordance with applicable law.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These include:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Social Media Cookies: Enable sharing on platforms like Facebook and YouTube

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law:

  • Transaction records: Retained for up to 5 years for legal and accounting purposes
  • Inquiries and communications: Retained for up to 2 years after the last interaction
  • Vehicle photos and listings: Retained for the duration of the listing and up to 1 year after

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

7. Your Rights Under Philippine Law

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to be Informed — You have the right to know how your data is collected, used, and stored
  • Right to Access — You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to Rectify — You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to Erasure — You may request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Right to Block/Remove — You may object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to Data Portability — You may request transfer of your data to another service provider
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 10.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party services, including:

  • Facebook and Messenger (for inquiries and page interactions)
  • YouTube (for video content)
  • Google Maps (for location services)
  • EmailJS (for contact form submissions)

These services have their own privacy policies, and GTACarsPH is not responsible for their data practices. We encourage you to review their policies before using these services.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or operational needs. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Notify you through our website or social media channels

We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
