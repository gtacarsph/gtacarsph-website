Seller Tips

How to Prepare Your Car for Sale

Follow these simple steps to get the best offer for your vehicle.

01

Clean & Detail

Give your car a thorough wash inside and out. A clean car not only looks better but shows you've taken good care of it, which can increase its perceived value.

02

Gather Documents

Prepare your original OR/CR, deed of sale (if applicable), and any maintenance records. Complete paperwork builds trust and speeds up the transaction.

03

Take Great Photos

Capture clear, well-lit photos of your car from multiple angles — front, rear, sides, interior, dashboard, and engine bay. Good photos help us give you a more accurate offer.
